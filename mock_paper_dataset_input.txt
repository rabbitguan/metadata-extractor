Paper and Dataset Notes for Metadata Extraction

Title: A Multi-Source Urban Water Quality Dataset for Seasonal Analysis

Overview:
This study presents a multi-source urban water quality dataset collected from 18 monitoring sites across a large metropolitan area. The paper focuses on seasonal variation, water chemistry indicators, and spatial differences between upstream, midstream, and downstream locations.

Authors:
Li Ming, Chen Yu, Wang Tao, and Emily Zhang
Corresponding author: emily.zhang@example.edu

Publication:
Journal of Environmental Data Science
2025, Volume 12, Issue 3, pages 88-112
DOI: 10.1234/jeds.2025.0318

Dataset:
The dataset includes CSV tables, calibration logs, sensor metadata, sampling schedules, and a README describing column definitions.
File formats: CSV, TXT, XLSX, JSON
Download page: https://data.example.org/water-quality

Methods:
Samples were collected monthly between March 2022 and February 2024.
Measurements include pH, dissolved oxygen, conductivity, turbidity, nitrate, phosphate, ammonia nitrogen, and temperature.
Quality control followed a two-stage validation process with manual review and automated outlier detection.

Keywords:
urban water quality, seasonal analysis, monitoring network, dataset, environmental sensing, spatiotemporal variation

Usage Notes:
Users should cite both the article and the dataset.
The dataset license is CC BY 4.0.
Missing values are marked as NA.
The dataset is intended for reproducibility studies, baseline model training, and environmental trend analysis.
